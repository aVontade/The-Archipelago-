Getting my first house in Annadel was bittersweet.
It wasn't a clean movie.
In fact, at the time when I was living in western native township,
my brother Rashid got notice from the government
that he had opportunity to buy a house in Annadel.
And if we want to qualify, you had to go on a waiting list
as much as five, six years before you got the opportunity.
He got his opportunity and he said now he didn't want to take it
because he wanted to stay close to the family and the friends
or the drinking was taking place.
I made a deal with him to take his opportunity,
move into his house and when my house would become available,
that we would swap back again, which he agreed to.
That was my first move to Annadel when my brother got his house.
I was in the house for a year when he visited me with his wife
and jealousy, jealousy is something else.
She wanted the place because she could see we were very comfortable
and a massive argument and suit between me and my brother Rashid
and eventually I had to move out back into his flat
so he could take his house and my mum had to intervene
because we were almost at blows with each other.
So it was bittersweet.
But a year later I got my opportunity after pulling some strings
and I could buy my own place and it was really an amazing place
to have your own house.
I made some improvements to the place and when I got my offer
to move to Cape Town, I came to Cape Town with a sizable amount
of money in my bank account.
Good job and I could buy where I wanted to buy.
